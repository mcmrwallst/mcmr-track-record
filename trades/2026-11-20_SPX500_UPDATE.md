## What happened: 6–20 November

**Week of 9 Nov: relief rally.** The S\&P and Nasdaq posted their first weekly gain in four weeks. CoreWeave's results helped AI stocks find a footing mid-week.

**12 Nov: Jensen Huang resigns as Nvidia CEO.** He left as CEO and board member, effective immediately, after reports of personal misconduct. CFO Colette Kress became interim CEO. Nvidia fell almost 14% at the open and closed down about 4%. The S\&P only fell 0.35% to 7,592.40. On the day, the market treated it as a company event, not a market event.

**13 Nov: inflation.** October PPI came in at +6.5% y/y, the highest since November 2022. Fed officials have said they are open to a more aggressive hiking path. The policy condition in the thesis is getting stronger, not weaker.

**16–18 Nov: Nvidia earnings fail to hold.** The post-earnings rally faded and Nvidia turned lower. Salesforce fell about 10% in a software sell-off. The S\&P fell five sessions in a row, its longest losing streak since June.

**18–19 Nov: Anthropic IPO.** Priced at $83, above the $70–78 range, and reported as several times oversubscribed. The raise was about $68bn plus a $26bn private placement, at a valuation of about $1.5tn. ANT rose about 17% on its first day. **The index did not follow.** The S\&P was up as much as 1.3% in the morning, then closed down 2.02%, with more than 90% of its constituents lower.

**20 Nov: correction.** The S\&P is down about 3.5% intraday and more than 10% below its record. The Nasdaq has erased its gain for 2026. The S\&P is on course for a seventh straight loss, the longest streak since 2020. Nvidia is down about 39% from 6 Oct.

## The correlation flip: the pre-registered condition is met

**Convention (unchanged from the 3 Nov update):** daily S\&P 500 returns against daily 10-year Treasury *price* returns, taken from the 10-year par yield. Positive means stocks and bonds move together (the supply / term-premium regime). Negative means bonds hedge stocks (the demand / growth regime). Recomputing 29 Oct with this method gives −0.46 for the 20-day, the same figure published on 3 Nov.

On 3 Nov I wrote that I would count the regime as shifted **only when both the 20-day and the 60-day are below zero.**

|Window|9 Oct|29 Oct|5 Nov|19 Nov|
|-|-|-|-|-|
|20-day|+0.51|−0.46|−0.34|**−0.40**|
|60-day|+0.45|+0.18|0.00|**−0.11**|

*Table: 10-year, computed from published closes through 19 Nov. 20 Nov is not in the table because the Treasury does not publish the day's yields until after the close.*

**Both windows are below zero. By the rule I pre-registered, the regime has shifted.** This is the flip the 21 Sep thesis predicted. For most of this year stocks and long bonds sold off together on fiscal supply and term premium, which was the regime my ZN/ZB term-premium trades were built on. An AI capex slowdown is a demand story, and under a demand story bonds go back to hedging stocks. The 60-day crossed zero around 6–9 Nov and has stayed below.

## Interpretation (opinion, not data)

The Anthropic debut is the most useful information in this period. The largest AI equity listing of the cycle priced above range, traded well, and **the rest of the AI complex was sold into it.** Investors used the event to take profit, not to add exposure. In my reading, a market that can't rally on its best news is distributing.

The thesis's 2018 lesson was that the market topped on good news. This fortnight had the best AI news of the year: a strong debut and a Nvidia beat. The index went down on both.
