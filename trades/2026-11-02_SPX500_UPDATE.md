## What has happened since entry

- The S&P fell 1.8% on Friday 30 Oct to 7580.07, then gave up early gains on Monday 2 Nov to close at 7541.40.
- **The driver is an AI and semiconductor valuation unwind, not a broad policy or liquidity shock.** Tech has led the move lower. Energy and old-economy names have held up.
- **Credit has partially confirmed.** HY OAS was about 2.68% around entry and about 3.3% in late October. CCC spreads are leading the widening. This is the pre-registered confirmation signal, moving in the thesis direction.
- **Breadth does not confirm.** On Monday most S&P constituents rose while the index fell. On Friday new 52-week highs still far outnumbered new lows. In Q4 2018 the selling was broad. Right now it is concentrated in one sector.

**Assessment:** The position is working. The thesis is only partially confirmed: credit says yes, breadth says not yet.

## Decision: hold through the midterms

I am holding the position through the 2026-11-03 elections. I am not reducing size or hedging into the event.

**Reasoning:** The thesis is a Q4-2018 analog driven by tightening financial conditions. It is not a bet on the election result. Closing because of an event the thesis doesn't name would turn a macro position into an event trade.

**What I'm watching for:** In the 2018 analog, the index rallied after the midterms and then kept falling into December. A post-election relief rally is therefore consistent with the analog, as long as it holds below the stop. A rally is not invalidation on its own.
